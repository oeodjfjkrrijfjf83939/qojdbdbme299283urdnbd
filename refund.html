Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026

Service Type

MultiLynk QR is a digital profile management service. All services are delivered digitally through cloud-based platform.

Cancellation Policy

For subscription-based plans (monthly, yearly, or custom plans), there is a specific cancellation process:

  • Contact our support team via email or WhatsApp
  • Provide your account details and reason for cancellation
  • Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period

Refund Policy

Refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Any refund request will be processed only after careful consideration and discussion between the service provider and the client.

We aim to reach a fair mutual agreement in all cases. Please Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
