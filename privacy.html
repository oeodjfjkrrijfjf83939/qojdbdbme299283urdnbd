Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Data Protection

We take your privacy seriously. All user data is securely stored in the cloud with industry-standard encryption and security measures.

Information Usage

The information we collect is strictly for the purpose of creating your custom MultiLynk QR digital profile.

Please note that the information required (such as contact details, social links, certificates, and forms) is effectively public information - the kind you would typically share on a business card or professional profile.

Therefore, there is no need to worry about the exposure of highly sensitive private data.

Data Storage

Your profile data is stored securely in the cloud. Admin accounts have role-based access controls, and each user's account is protected with a unique login credentials.
